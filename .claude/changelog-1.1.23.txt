The Starcade is open: walk into the arcade for a whole new indoor area. A pink arrow shows first-time visitors the door.
In the full game you can recruit Maya and Leo inside the Starcade for 1000 coins each.
Friends you find in hide and seek are now twice as strong. Full-game players no longer get friends from doorbells.
Earn the full game by playing: it unlocks for free once you have earned more than 15,000 Essence.
Phones: tap anywhere to ring a doorbell or pick something up, and use a second finger to aim.
The corner map is see-through, and pumpkins on the ground no longer block your way.
New talents screen layout, a new look for the tornado, and many small fixes.
