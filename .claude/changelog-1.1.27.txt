New: a starting tutorial. Professor Fizz talks you through your first night on a walkie-talkie: your first trick or treat, a free spin of the candy machine and a free gun upgrade. You can skip it or turn it off.
New: 24 waves, each with its own boss and its own two kinds of helpers. Twenty new bosses join the first four. After wave 24 something enormous arrives that cannot be beaten: last as long as you can.
The shop now brings back a friend who is out for the night, and can reroll the lines on a gun. It no longer sells treats.
The candy machine now has some duds: about one spin in five wins nothing.
Cars can now be blown up by everyone, with any gun.
The Starcade closes for the night after wave 5.
Hide and Shriek is taken out for now. Every night starts the same way.
Essence: you earn a little less, and nothing for starting and leaving. Talents further from the centre cost more.
In the RUN apps you can watch an ad to take the locked prize in a candy bowl for that run.
Fixed: a level-up no longer pops up in the middle of ringing a doorbell.
