A new talent constellation, the Buddy System: fifteen talents that make the friends at your side stronger.
After a boss you now get 20 quiet seconds to find the shop. The shop no longer opens by itself.
Guns you find now come at about your own level instead of level 1.
Rarity colours are clearer: yellow-green, deep blue, purple and orange. Gun inscriptions use the same colours.
Very rarely a gun carries an orange legendary inscription.
When you pick up a gun you can compare it with either gun you carry before you swap. This also works on phones.
Big chains of blasts are lighter on the screen and run more smoothly.
