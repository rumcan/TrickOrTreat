When a boss is beaten it drops a magnet: every coin and candy in town flies to that spot so you can collect it all there.
Coins, candy and treat bags left on the ground now disappear after one minute. They blink before they go.
Houses give treats again only after you have visited every house in town and the three stands in the Starcade.
When a boss arrives, the other monsters leave. Each boss now fights with a bigger group of its own helpers.
New treat: Long-Distance Dedication. Much stronger against monsters far away, weaker against monsters up close.
Temple Idol is new: the more coins you carry, the stronger you are.
Friends walk smoothly beside you. In hide and seek, a friend who falls gets back up after 20 seconds.
New setting to hide the names above monsters. The bars above monsters are smaller.
The Starcade door now says ENTER HERE.
