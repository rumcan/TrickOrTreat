The Hollow Beyond can now be beaten. It is the toughest fight in town, and bringing it down starts the night over at wave 1 with stronger monsters: lieutenants and captains, then captains and mayors, climbing for five nights. Captains and higher ranks cast spells.
Each Hollow Beyond saves a checkpoint, so a new run can start at wave 26 and beyond.
Levelling up now refills your health and shield. Reward picks come from bosses instead, with far better odds of Epic and Legendary guns.
