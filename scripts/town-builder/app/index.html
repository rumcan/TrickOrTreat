Town overview

Build your town

1. Add objects
Choose a picture on the left, then click the map to place a new copy.

2. Edit objects
Choose Select / edit, then click an object. The panel changes only your selection.

3. Draw ground
Open Lots & paving. Choose Draw new or Paint a shape. To change an existing area, choose Edit existing.

4. See finished ground
Ground changes appear as outlines first. Rebuild ground renders the finished surfaces. Objects appear immediately.

Keyboard shortcuts

VSelect / move. Shift-click adds to the selection, drag moves everything selected.
BPaint the chosen sprite. Drag with a spacing set to paint rows or scatter trees and leaves.
Space / right-drag / HPan. Mouse wheel zooms at the cursor.
★ PresetsStamp a fixed designed arrangement. Positions, sizes, facing and item choices stay identical on every click. Scale resizes the entire area. Select several sprites and press Save as preset to preserve your exact layout.
SToggle base shadows.
FMirror the brush or the selection (fronts face +X instead of +Y). Mirroring also reverses sign lettering, so keep shops with signs unmirrored.
[ ]Smaller / bigger (brush or selection). Multi-selection has Resize (%) and spacing controls in the Selection panel.
ArrowsNudge selection ¼ m (Shift: 2 m) along the ground grid.
{ / }Smaller / bigger in big 25% steps (selection or brush).
Home / EndBring to front / move to back.
+ / - (or ] / [)Bigger / smaller (selection or brush).
. / , (or PgUp / PgDn)Move forward one / back one: past the next sprite it overlaps.
Ctrl+G / Ctrl+Shift+GGroup / ungroup the selection. Clicking any member selects the whole group; Alt-click picks one member. Stamped presets are grouped automatically.
Ctrl+C / Ctrl+VCopy the selection / paste it at the mouse (paste as many times as you like).
Ctrl+DDuplicate the selection.
DelDelete the selection.
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo / redo.
Alt-clickPick: in Paint mode, Alt-click a placed sprite to use it as the brush.
R K LGround: Roads (choose Build road or Remove road and drag across the grid), Lots & paving (drag a rectangle; choose car park, plaza, pavement patch, yard or field; choose Edit existing to select, move or delete one), Reflections. Then Rebuild ground.
TWalk as the kid: WASD or arrows walk at game speed (Shift runs, Alt walks through objects, click jumps there), C changes kid, Esc stops. The camera, zoom, night and the kid's torch are the game's, so the town looks as it will in the game.
YLight spots: click to place a bare light anywhere (no object needed), set its radius, strength and kind; drag to move, Shift-click or Del to remove. They go to the game with the next export.
JPaint streetlights as ordinary sprites. Select, move or delete them without rebaking. Their reflections are independent.
G P OGrid, place labels, plan overlay.

Everything saves automatically to output/town-builder/layout.json (sprites) and plans/maple-falls.json (ground).

Build together

Each person has their own view and Undo history. If both change the same setting, you choose which change to keep. Roads and lots appear as outlines until either person presses Rebuild ground.
